# Wholesale outreach playbook

**Goal:** turn small crystal shops abroad into repeat buyers of Earth Editions Trade stock by the kilo: spheres, palm stones, hearts, tumbles and Himalayan quartz. One good shop that reorders every 6–8 weeks is worth more than 50 Etsy orders.

**Offer:** trade.eartheditions.co. A shop applies, we approve it, and it sees trade prices. There's no minimum order. We send photos or a video of the actual lot before they pay.

## Who we contact (in this order)

1. **Etsy and online crystal shops in the US, UK, EU and Australia (Tier A/B).** They already sell the exact shapes we make, at retail prices. Most buy from US middlemen who buy from India. We are the source, so they get better margins and pieces they choose themselves. Contact them **only through the website, email or Instagram they publish**. Etsy forbids sales messages through Etsy Conversations, and it can close the Atyahara and EarthEditions shops for it.
2. **Walk-in crystal and metaphysical shops.** They need steady restocks of tumbles, palm stones and hearts, and want a few showpieces such as Himalayan points and large spheres. Pitch both.
3. **Live sellers (Whatnot, TikTok Live, Instagram Live).** They move tumbles, mini hearts and mystery bags by the kilo. Pitch mixed kilo lots.
4. **Japan and Taiwan.** These buyers care most about quality, consistency and a long relationship, and above all about Himalayan quartz: offer Manihar and Manikaran quartz and golden rutile lots. Write to Japan in polite Japanese, start with a short introduction and no hard sell, and offer to send photos. Many buy at the Tokyo Mineral Show (Sunshine City, Ikebukuro) and on SUPER DELIVERY, Japan's wholesale marketplace. **We're at the Tokyo Mineral Show from 11 December 2026**, and the Japanese and Taiwanese drafts say so until the show ends: invite anyone who replies to come and see the stones. Cold email to Japan is slow, so the show is where most of these leads will turn into orders.

## How to send

- **Email, one at a time:** use **Open in Gmail** on the lead, read it, change anything that sounds wrong, send it, then click **Mark email sent**.
- **Email, several at once:** tick the shops (or **Select all**) and press **Send**. They go out from the ERP's Gmail one by one, about half a minute apart, so keep the page open until it says Finished. Read a few of the drafts first. Each shop is checked before its email goes: if it's already a customer, a trade buyer, on the Omnisend list, or anywhere in our Gmail, it's skipped and the reason is noted. Due follow-ups can be sent the same way. A shop that replied or bounced is skipped and moved to Replied or Bounced.
- **Instagram DM** from @eartheditions_ when a shop has no email, or for live sellers. Open their profile, like one or two recent posts, then send the DM. Then click **Mark DM sent**.
- **At most 20 new shops by email and 12 new DMs a day.** Follow-ups don't count toward the 20, but the ERP stops at 60 emails a day in total. Sending more gets the mailbox flagged as spam and the Instagram account blocked from sending DMs. If the mailbox has never sent emails like these, start with 10 a day for the first week.
- **No attachments or price lists in the first message.** Use plain text and one link. Attachments from strangers go to spam.
- **Follow-ups:** one after 4 days, and a last one 6 days after that, both in the same thread. After that, stop. The Today tab shows them when they're due.
- **Reply the same day** to anyone who answers. Ask what they sell most, then send 3–5 photos of matching lots from stock.

## Rules (legal and reputation)

- Every email says who we are, where we are (Mumbai), and how to opt out ("just reply 'no thanks' and I won't write again"). If someone says no, mark them **Not interested** and never contact them again.
- **Germany and Austria:** the law there (UWG) makes cold sales email to businesses risky. Use Instagram DM or the shop's contact form, not email. The drafts already follow this.
- **Japan:** emailing a shop's published business address is allowed, unless the site says it refuses sales email (広告メールお断り / 営業メールお断り). If it does, mark the lead **Skip**.
- **UK and EU:** write only to business addresses (info@, hello@, shop@), and only about wholesale, which is relevant to their business. Delete their details if they ask.
- Never send to existing trade buyers or past customers from this list. Those go through Trade Site and Raj. The list is checked against them when it's built, and again right before each email.
- Never promise a price, discount or delivery date that Sir hasn't approved.

## What to track

The stage on each lead: Sent → Replied → In talks → Sample / trial → Customer. Add a note after every conversation, for example "wants 5 kg rose quartz tumbles, AAA only". We'll judge it after the first 200 contacts:

- **Healthy:** about 8–15% reply, and 2–4 trial orders.
- **If fewer than 5% reply:** change the first line and the subject, not the volume.
